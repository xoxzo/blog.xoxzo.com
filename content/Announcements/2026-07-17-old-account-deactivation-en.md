Summary: To improve the security of our services, we are introducing a new policy for inactive accounts. This policy will take effect on July 16, 2026.

Thank you for using Xoxzo.

To enhance the security of our services, we are introducing a new policy regarding inactive accounts.

Accounts with no login or service activity (API or Web) for a continuous period of 365 days may be deactivated.

Before an account is deactivated, we will send a notification to the registered email address. Simply signing in during the notification period will keep your account active.

This policy will take effect on **July 16, 2026**.

We have also updated our Terms of Service to reflect this change. You can review the updated Terms of Service here:

[Terms of Service](https://www.xoxzo.com/about/tos/)

Thank you for your continued support.
